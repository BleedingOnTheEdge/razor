# Vision

Razor aims to become the **default optimisation and monitoring layer for professional algorithmic
trading worldwide**. Whether a trader works through MetaTrader, cTrader, a cryptocurrency exchange or
a prime brokerage API, Razor is the intelligence layer that makes their trading systematic,
measurable and continuously improving - without requiring them to build or maintain any of it
themselves.

The roadmap beyond v1.0.0 LTS is tracked in `blueprint/future-features`.
